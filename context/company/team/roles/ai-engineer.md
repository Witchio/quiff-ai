# Sample role: AI engineer

**Status:** Sample role; not a confirmed team member.

## Purpose

Explore and build AI capabilities that improve the login/signup app, with results that users and engineers can inspect and trust.

## Example responsibilities

- Identify suitable user-facing AI capabilities and define their behavior.
- Ground model outputs in the product data and context available to the app.
- Evaluate relevance, accuracy, and uncertainty across representative use cases.
- Manage model cost, latency, and safe handling of user data.

## Example ownership

- AI feature behavior and evaluation
- Context assembly and output quality
- Model integration quality and observability

## Collaboration

Works with product and engineering teammates to deliver useful, appropriately qualified AI features.
