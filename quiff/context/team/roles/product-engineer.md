# Sample role: Product engineer

**Status:** Sample role; not a confirmed team member.

## Purpose

Build and improve the signup app based on product requirements and user needs.

## Example responsibilities

- Work with product and design to turn user needs into app behavior.
- Build and maintain the web experience and its application interfaces.
- Build and maintain application features and interfaces.
- Coordinate with engineering teammates on integration boundaries.

## Example ownership

- User-facing product behavior
- Frontend and application integration
- signup app features and acceptance criteria

## Collaboration

Works with product, design, and engineering teammates to deliver cohesive app behavior.
