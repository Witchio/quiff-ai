# Sample role: Platform engineer

**Status:** Sample role; not a confirmed team member.

## Purpose

Make the signup app's infrastructure, delivery pipeline, and integrations reliable and observable.

## Example responsibilities

- Maintain build, test, and deployment infrastructure for the app.
- Support reliable integrations used by the app.
- Design operational monitoring and alerting.
- Define retry, timeout, and error-handling behavior for external services.

## Example ownership

- Backend services and integration reliability
- Deployment and operational visibility
- Delivery and operational reliability for the app

## Collaboration

Works with product and engineering teammates to keep app delivery and operations reliable.
